# Cycles of Intelligence: A Multi-Dimensional Strategic Analysis of Global Sales Performance

## 📊 Project Overview
This Business Intelligence project analyzes over **113,037 transactions** for XYZ Corporation. The goal was to transform raw sales data into actionable strategic insights regarding product performance, regional growth, and customer demographics.

**Key Achievement:** Developed a holistic dashboard that identifies a total profit of **$32M** and total sales of **$95M**.

---

## 🛠️ Technical Implementation
* **Data Modeling:** Established a **Star Schema** consisting of one Fact table (`Sales`) and three Dimension tables (`Date`, `Customer`, `Product`) to optimize query performance.
* **DAX Engineering:** Authored custom DAX measures for complex ranking and profit margin analysis, including `Rank Bike Sales by Country` and `Top 5 Accessory Selling Countries`.
* **Interactive Visualization:** Designed a multi-page dashboard featuring drill-through filters for Country, Product Category, and Year.

---

## 💡 Key Business Insights
* **Core Profit Driver:** The **Bikes** category is the most lucrative, contributing **63.68%** ($21M) of total profit.
* **Market Dominance:** The **United States** leads global revenue at **$28M**, followed by Australia at $21M.
* **High-Value Segment:** **Adults (35-64)** represent the primary consumer base, particularly for high-margin Bike products.
* **Gender Balance:** Purchasing power is nearly equal between male (**52%**) and female (**48%**) customers.

---

## 🚀 Strategic Recommendations
1. **Concentrate Capital:** Increase inventory and marketing focus on the high-performing **Bikes** category in the US and Germany.
2. **Targeted Expansion:** Launch tailored campaigns for the **Clothing** segment in underperforming regions like France and Canada to diversify revenue.
3. **Customer Lifecycle:** Develop loyalty programs specifically for the **25–64 age group** to sustain long-term market control.
