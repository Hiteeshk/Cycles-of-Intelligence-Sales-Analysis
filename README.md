# Cycles of Intelligence: A Multi-Dimensional Strategic Analysis of Global Sales Performance

## 📊 Project Overview
[cite_start]This Business Intelligence project analyzes over **113,037 transactions** [cite: 92] for XYZ Corporation. [cite_start]The goal was to transform raw sales data into actionable strategic insights regarding product performance, regional growth, and customer demographics[cite: 51, 85].

[cite_start]**Key Achievement:** Developed a holistic dashboard that identifies a total profit of **$32M** and total sales of **$95M**[cite: 259, 260].

---

## 🛠️ Technical Implementation
* [cite_start]**Data Modeling:** Established a **Star Schema** consisting of one Fact table (`Sales`) and three Dimension tables (`Date`, `Customer`, `Product`) to optimize query performance[cite: 146, 147].
* [cite_start]**DAX Engineering:** Authored custom DAX measures for complex ranking and profit margin analysis, including `Rank Bike Sales by Country` and `Top 5 Accessory Selling Countries`[cite: 363, 385].
* [cite_start]**Interactive Visualization:** Designed a multi-page dashboard featuring drill-through filters for Country, Product Category, and Year[cite: 264, 268, 423].

---

## 💡 Key Business Insights
* [cite_start]**Core Profit Driver:** The **Bikes** category is the most lucrative, contributing **63.68%** ($21M) of total profit[cite: 177].
* [cite_start]**Market Dominance:** The **United States** leads global revenue at **$28M**, followed by Australia at $21M[cite: 165].
* [cite_start]**High-Value Segment:** **Adults (35-64)** represent the primary consumer base, particularly for high-margin Bike products[cite: 63, 174].
* [cite_start]**Gender Balance:** Purchasing power is nearly equal between male (**52%**) and female (**48%**) customers[cite: 168].

1.  [cite_start]**Concentrate Capital:** Increase inventory and marketing focus on the high-performing **Bikes** category in the US and Germany[cite: 69, 71].
2.  [cite_start]**Targeted Expansion:** Launch tailored campaigns for the **Clothing** segment in underperforming regions like France and Canada to diversify revenue[cite: 81, 83].
